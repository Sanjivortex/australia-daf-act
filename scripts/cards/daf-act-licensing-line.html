DAF Act licensing

Where the licensing line falls

Needs a licence

  • Operating a digital asset platform
  • Operating a tokenised custody platform
  • Dealing in or advising on platforms

Outside the licence

  • Small DAPs: up to $10m a year and $5,000 per client, with notice to ASIC
  • Public blockchains that pass 3 tests, and validating them
  • Technology-only providers

Depends on the facts

  • Who holds the keys
  • Native tokenised fund units
  • Pools and lending for tokenised products
