DAF Act calendar

When Australia's new platform licensing starts

8 Apr 2026

Royal Assent. The Act is law but not yet in force.

27 Nov 2026

ASIC consultation on draft standards (CP 392) closes.

8 Apr 2027

The Act commences and licence applications open.

7 Oct 2027

Last day for existing platforms to apply and keep operating.

Earlier ASIC material said 9 April 2027. ASIC's October 2026 consultation says 8 April 2027.
