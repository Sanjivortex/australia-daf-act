23DefinitionsA digital token is an electronic record that a person can factually control: transfer it, exclude others from transferring it, and demonstrate both.[1] DAF Acts 761GB(1), (2)"(a) transfer the electronic record; and (b) exclude one or more other persons from transferring the electronic record; and (c) demonstrate that the person can do the things in paragraphs (a) and (b)."
24DefinitionsA DAP is a facility under which an operator possesses digital tokens for or on behalf of a client.[1] DAF Acts 761GC(1), (2)"a person (the operator) possesses one or more digital tokens ... for or on behalf of another person"
25DefinitionsA TCP is a facility under which the operator holds a non-money asset and creates a single redeemable token for each asset; a TCP cannot be a DAP.[1] DAF Acts 761GD(1); s 761GC(4)"identifies one or more assets other than money ... the operator creates a single digital token ... confers a right to redeem, or direct the delivery of, the underlying asset"
26DefinitionsPublic digital token infrastructure must meet three protocol tests, and regulations can switch the test off or prescribe infrastructure.[1] DAF Acts 9E(2), (3)"(a) ... open source and publicly accessible standards ... in a non-discretionary manner; (b) ... permit any person to contribute ...; (c) ... not reliant on any participant having a role so critical ...; (d) no regulations are made ..."
27DefinitionsFor a wrapped token, the redemption right is disregarded when deciding whether it is a financial product.[1] DAF Acts 765E(2)"Disregard the redemption right when working out whether the wrapped token is ... a financial product."
28LicensingDAP and TCP services must be provided by a constitutionally-covered corporation.[1] DAF Acts 766M; s 9 definition"the person is not a constitutionally-covered corporation ... This section is a civil penalty provision"
29LicensingA constitutionally-covered corporation is broadly a trading, financial or foreign corporation.[2] Constitutions 51(xx); Act s 9 definition"Foreign corporations, and trading or financial corporations formed within the limits of the Commonwealth"
30LicensingThe voting policy and DAP/TCP Guide are required before a platform is issued to a retail client; communications about underlying assets must be passed on when a client asks.[1] DAF Actss 1020AN(1), 1020AP(1), 1020AR(1)"before issuing a digital asset platform or tokenised custody platform to a person as a retail client ... ensure that there is a policy (the voting policy) ... must, on request, ensure the client is given a copy of such communications"
31LicensingRelated services (dealing other than issuing, advice) also need licensing.[9] ASIC CP 392Para 41"Related financial services will also require an AFS licence (e.g. to deal in other than issue, or provide advice about DAPs and TCPs)."
32LicensingLicensees must comply with asset-holding and transactional and settlement standards and have compliant platform rules.[1] DAF Actss 912BB, 912BC"The licensee must comply with the following standards ... The licensee must have platform rules for the platform that comply with subsection 912BG(1)."
33LicensingPlatform rules must include transparent and non-discriminatory eligibility criteria.[1] DAF Acts 912BG(1)(a)"transparent and non-discriminatory eligibility criteria for identifying who can become a client under the platform"
34LicensingA DAP/TCP Guide must be given before issuing a platform to a retail client.[1] DAF Acts 1020AN(1)"The licensee must, before issuing a digital asset platform or tokenised custody platform to a person as a retail client under the platform, give the person a DAP/TCP Guide"
35LicensingAn issuer that must prepare a DAP/TCP Guide must also prepare a target market determination.[10] ASIC draft RG 000Section D table, design and distribution obligations"An issuer must prepare a TMD for a DAP or TCP if it is required to prepare a DAP/TCP Guide."
36ExemptionsLow-value DAP exemption: no financial products held, transactions not over $10 million in 12 months, client entry values not over $5,000, and a notice lodged with ASIC.[1] DAF Acts 911A(2)(ja)"(ii) the total value of transactions in the last 12 months through all relevant platforms does not exceed $10 million; and (iii) ... does not exceed $5,000; and (iv) the person has lodged with ASIC, in the prescribed form, notice"
37ExemptionsRegulations can raise the exemption amounts; ASIC says the tests rise with inflation.[10] ASIC draft RG 000Table 3; Act s 911A(4B)"The client value and transaction value tests increase over time with the rate of inflation."
