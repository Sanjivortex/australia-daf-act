How this was checked

  • The law is taken from the Act as made on the Federal Register of Legislation, read in full.
  • Dates and implementation steps are taken from Parliament's bill page and ASIC's own publications, including Consultation Paper 392 of 15 October 2026 and its draft regulatory guide.
  • Statements about Tokeniser and Redbelly are taken from their own websites and documentation, and are presented as company statements, not findings.
  • Each link was opened on 27 October 2026. The results of the automated link check are below.
  • The repository runs automatic checks on every change: each citation must point to a listed source, every listed source must be cited, the "not legal advice" statement must appear on every page, each X post must fit in 280 characters, and no page may describe Redbelly, Tokeniser or an issuer as licensed or compliant.

DefinitionsA digital token is an electronic record that a person can factually control: transfer it, exclude others from transferring it, and demonstrate both.[1]s 761GB(1), (2)“(a) transfer the electronic record; and (b) exclude one or more other persons from transferring the electronic record; and (c) demonstrate that the person can do the things in paragraphs (a) and (b).”
DefinitionsA DAP is a facility under which an operator possesses digital tokens for or on behalf of a client.[1]s 761GC(1), (2)“a person (the operator) possesses one or more digital tokens ... for or on behalf of another person”
DefinitionsA TCP is a facility under which the operator holds a non-money asset and creates a single redeemable token for each asset; a TCP cannot be a DAP.[1]s 761GD(1); s 761GC(4)“identifies one or more assets other than money ... the operator creates a single digital token ... confers a right to redeem, or direct the delivery of, the underlying asset”
DefinitionsPublic digital token infrastructure must meet three protocol tests, and regulations can switch the test off or prescribe infrastructure.[1]s 9E(2), (3)“(a) ... open source and publicly accessible standards ... in a non-discretionary manner; (b) ... permit any person to contribute ...; (c) ... not reliant on any participant having a role so critical ...; (d) no regulations are made ...”
DefinitionsFor a wrapped token, the redemption right is disregarded when deciding whether it is a financial product.[1]s 765E(2)“Disregard the redemption right when working out whether the wrapped token is ... a financial product.”
LicensingDAP and TCP services must be provided by a constitutionally-covered corporation.[1]s 766M; s 9 definition“the person is not a constitutionally-covered corporation ... This section is a civil penalty provision”
LicensingA constitutionally-covered corporation is broadly a trading, financial or foreign corporation.[2]s 51(xx); Act s 9 definition“Foreign corporations, and trading or financial corporations formed within the limits of the Commonwealth”
LicensingThe voting policy and DAP/TCP Guide are required before a platform is issued to a retail client; communications about underlying assets must be passed on when a client asks.[1]ss 1020AN(1), 1020AP(1), 1020AR(1)“before issuing a digital asset platform or tokenised custody platform to a person as a retail client ... ensure that there is a policy (the voting policy) ... must, on request, ensure the client is given a copy of such communications”
LicensingRelated services (dealing other than issuing, advice) also need licensing.[9]Para 41“Related financial services will also require an AFS licence (e.g. to deal in other than issue, or provide advice about DAPs and TCPs).”
LicensingLicensees must comply with asset-holding and transactional and settlement standards and have compliant platform rules.[1]ss 912BB, 912BC“The licensee must comply with the following standards ... The licensee must have platform rules for the platform that comply with subsection 912BG(1).”
LicensingPlatform rules must include transparent and non-discriminatory eligibility criteria.[1]s 912BG(1)(a)“transparent and non-discriminatory eligibility criteria for identifying who can become a client under the platform”
LicensingA DAP/TCP Guide must be given before issuing a platform to a retail client.[1]s 1020AN(1)“The licensee must, before issuing a digital asset platform or tokenised custody platform to a person as a retail client under the platform, give the person a DAP/TCP Guide”

What was not checked

  • The Explanatory Memorandum paragraphs cited on Tokeniser's website (for example EM 1.338, 1.369 and Example 1.26) were not relied on. The EM is linked from the Parliament bill page for readers who want to check them.
  • Whether Redbelly's protocol software is fully open-source and publicly accessible (one element of s 9E(2)(a)) was not independently verified.
  • No search of ASIC's professional registers was made. The explainer makes no statement about whether any company holds an AFSL.
  • Company performance figures on the Tokeniser and Redbelly websites were not used and were not verified.
